Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the AirAware demonstration website handles information when you use its air-quality search and monitoring features. AirAware is an informational project and is not an emergency or medical service.

2. Information you provide

If you search for a location, the location name is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API to find coordinates. If you choose to use your device location, your browser asks for permission and the coordinates are used to request nearby modeled air-quality information. Location access is optional.

3. Local storage

Saved locations are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to an AirAware account server by this version of the application. You can clear them by removing the saved locations or clearing this site's browser storage.

4. Third-party services

AirAware retrieves location search and air-quality data from Open-Meteo. The interactive map uses OpenStreetMap map tiles. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser makes requests. Their own privacy terms apply to their services.

5. Notifications

If you enable browser notifications, your browser manages the permission. This version does not run a background monitoring service and cannot guarantee alerts while the website is closed. A notification may be displayed when the dashboard is open and a threshold condition is detected.

6. Data accuracy and retention

Air-quality values may be modeled estimates and may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. AirAware does not operate the underlying monitoring network. We do not maintain an AirAware user account database in this frontend demonstration.

7. Security

Use an up-to-date browser and device. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this site.

8. Children's privacy

This site is intended as a general public information tool and is not specifically directed at children. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. Replace this paragraph with the project owner's verified contact details before public launch. Do not publish invented contact information.
